The programs, in detail

Four programs of 4 to 10 weeks, worked on your rooms, your light and your own plants.

Program 01 · The two things that matter

Light & water

We map the light in each of your rooms — direction, distance from the window, obstructions — and place your plants against it. Then watering: checking weight and soil rather than following a schedule, and choosing pots and mixes that forgive mistakes.

  • 6 weeks
  • Beginners
  • 1 hour a week
  • Your phone camera

Program 02 · Read the leaves

Problems & pests

Send photographs — whole plant, close-up of the damage, and the soil surface. Yellow lower leaves, crispy edges and sudden drooping each mean different things. We identify pests properly and treat them with the least aggressive thing that works, following label directions.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 1 hour a week
  • Clear photos

Program 03 · More plants, free

Repotting & propagation

Repotting is done too often and at the wrong time by most people. We cover the signs that it's genuinely needed, how to check roots, pot sizing, and then propagation — water, soil and division — so your collection grows without spending anything.

  • 6 weeks
  • Some experience
  • 2 hours a week
  • Pots and mix

Weeks 1–2 · The shift

Weeks 3–4 · Air and light

Humidity, radiators and whether a lamp is worth it.

Program 04 · The hard months

Seasons & winter

Most plant deaths happen between November and March, when light drops and radiators dry the air. We adjust watering, humidity and feeding for the season, and cover grow lights honestly — which ones help, and where they're unnecessary.

  • Any level
  • 1 hour a week
  • Your winter rooms
